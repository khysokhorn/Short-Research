# Stage 0 — Input

## Topic

Why your brain freezes when you eat ice cream too fast

## Requested runtime

45 seconds

## Style

Funny microscopic body disaster

## Audience

General YouTube Shorts audience

## Format

9:16 vertical

## Creative intent

Make the inside of the mouth and head feel like a tiny emergency-control room. The comedy should come from exaggerated microscopic workers reacting to the sudden cold while the narration remains scientifically careful.

## Hard constraints

- hook in roughly the first 1.5 seconds
- no greeting or intro
- visual change/pattern interrupt every few seconds
- concrete visual instructions, not generic B-roll
- preserve uncertainty around the exact brain-freeze mechanism
- end with a useful payoff: why warming the roof of the mouth and eating slowly may help
- do not push to ArcReel
