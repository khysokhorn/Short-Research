# MAMA'S WINGS — continuity-first example structure

This is an original structural example for the `silent-rescue` preset. It is not a copied shot list from any reference video.

## Cast lock

- **Mother Hen** — chestnut-brown feathers, cream chest, red comb.
- **Pip** — smallest chick, pale yellow, short tuft leaning left.
- **Pop** — round golden-yellow chick, tiny darker patch over right eye.
- **Peep** — tall fluffy yellow chick, two upright head tufts.

Exactly these four characters persist throughout. Never add, remove, merge, duplicate, recolor, or redesign a chick.

## Connected geography

One orchard dirt path runs beside one shallow mossy stone irrigation channel. A fallen burning branch blocks the path. The same channel passes a cracked iron water pipe and ends at one old stone arch. The family moves forward along this route without teleporting.

## Beat chain

1. **Danger-first hook** — Fire is already chasing the family. A burning branch blocks the path.
2. **Decision** — Mother chooses the irrigation channel because it is the only visible route under the branch.
3. **Discovery** — The channel is dry; Pip notices a dripping, leaf-clogged pipe.
4. **Action and consequence** — Mother clears the pipe. Water flows through the established channel and dampens embers ahead.
5. **Near-failure** — A new falling ember/branch briefly separates Pip from the group. This danger must arise from the already-established wildfire, not from a new unexplained location.
6. **Hero beat** — Mother turns back, retrieves Pip, and rejoins Pop and Peep.
7. **Payoff** — All four cross beneath the same stone arch. Mother visibly verifies all three chicks are present, then shelters them under her wings.

Every adjacent beat must pass the WHY-NEXT test: the next event is caused or motivated by the previous event.
